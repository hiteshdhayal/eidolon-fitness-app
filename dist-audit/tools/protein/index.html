02 — The method

How this works.

I set a target in grams per kilogram of body weight, based on how many days a week you train and your goal: more for building muscle or more training days, a little more again when you're cutting calories to protect muscle, and a floor for anyone 50 or over.

Then I multiply that by your body weight. Every plan I build gets this from food first — dal, curd, paneer, chana, sprouts, rajma, eggs or meat — and only mentions a supplement if food alone genuinely can't close the gap.

03 — Questions

Fair questions.

Do I need protein powder to hit this? + –

No. Dal, curd, paneer, chana, sprouts, rajma, eggs or meat get most people there from food alone. A powder is only ever an optional top-up, never a requirement.

Why does training change the number? + –

More training days means more muscle repair work, so the target per kg of body weight goes up — up to a point. Losing fat also nudges the number up slightly, to protect muscle while you eat less.

Why does age matter? + –

People 50 and over absorb and use protein a little less efficiently, so I don't let the target drop below about 1 g per kg, even at a low training load.
