The short version

Some articles on this site — mostly the ones comparing products like protein powders or supplements — contain affiliate links. I earn a commission if you buy through these links, at no extra cost to you. The price you pay is the same whether you use my link or go to the site directly.

Amazon Associates

As an Amazon Associate, I earn from qualifying purchases made through links to Amazon on this site.

How I pick products

I don't pick a product just because it pays the highest commission. Before I recommend anything, I check:

  • Whether it's been tested by an independent, third-party lab
  • Whether the brand holds a valid FSSAI licence
  • What's actually in it — the ingredient list and quantities, not just the marketing claims

This follows the Advertising Standards Council of India's (ASCI) guidelines for influencer advertising — if a post contains an affiliate link, it's labelled, on the page and here.

No paid placements

I don't run paid product placements without a clear label. If a brand ever pays for a mention, that post will say so plainly — the same ASCI guidelines apply to that as they do to affiliate links.

Prices

Product pages on this site don't show prices, because they change often and I'd rather send you to the retailer's page for the current price than show you something out of date.
