Exercise · Intermediate

Push-up

The full push-up from the floor, in one straight line.

  • Chest
  • Triceps
  • Core
  • No equipment

Also called: Press-up

01 — How to

Do it right.

  1. Step 1

    Start in a plank with your hands under your shoulders.

  2. Step 2

    Lower your chest towards the floor, elbows at about 45 degrees.

  3. Step 3

    Push back up without letting the hips sag.

  4. Step 4

    Keep the body straight the whole way.

Cues

  • Squeeze the glutes and the core
  • Push the floor away

Common mistakes

  • Hips sagging or piking
  • Neck craning forward
