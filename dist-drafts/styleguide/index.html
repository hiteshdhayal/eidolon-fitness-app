09 — Media and motion

Photos and movement

Photos are real, grayscale and blended into the page. There are no boxes, frames or placeholders.

Photo treatment

Build-time grayscale, cool tint, high contrast. Cut-out photos fade at the bottom edge; photos with a background fade left, bottom, top and right. Never a visible rectangle.

Motion

One kinetic band per page. Headline lines rise once on load. Section reveals use scroll-driven CSS. Everything is static with reduced motion.
