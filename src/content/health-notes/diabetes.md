---
title: "Diabetes or prediabetes"
appliesTo: ["condition:diabetes"]
priority: 10
reviewedBy: ""
---
- Eat at regular times, and don't skip meals.
- Skip fruit juice and sweetened drinks; have whole fruit in sensible portions instead.
- Spread carbs across the day and pair them with protein or fibre (dal, curd, sabzi).
- If you take insulin or diabetes tablets, eating less can cause low sugar. Talk to your doctor before cutting calories.
