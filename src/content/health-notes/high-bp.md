- Keep salt under 5 g a day (about 1 level teaspoon, including what's already in food).
- Go easy on pickles, papad, namkeen and packaged foods; they carry most of the hidden salt.
- Eat more vegetables, fruit and dals.
- Keep taking your BP medicine as prescribed.
