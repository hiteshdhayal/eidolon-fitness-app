title: "Vitamin D or B12 deficiency"
appliesTo: ["condition:vitaminDB12"]

reviewedBy: ""
---
- Vitamin D: 15–20 minutes of midday sun on arms and face helps; eggs and fatty fish add a little.
- Vitamin B12: milk, curd, paneer, eggs, fish and meat. It's hard to get enough from plant food alone.
- Ask your doctor about testing and dosing before you start any supplement.
