---
title: "Low iron / anaemia"
appliesTo: ["condition:anaemia"]
priority: 45
reviewedBy: ""
---
- Eat iron-rich foods daily: dals, rajma, chana, green leafy vegetables, and eggs or meat if you eat them.
- Pair them with vitamin C — amla, guava, lemon or orange — to absorb more iron.
- Keep tea and coffee at least an hour away from meals.
- If your doctor has prescribed iron tablets, take them as advised.
