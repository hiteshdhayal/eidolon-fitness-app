- Have protein and fibre at every meal: dal, curd, paneer, eggs, sprouts and sabzi.
- Pick lower-GI grains more often: millets (bajra, jowar, ragi), whole wheat and oats.
- Keep sweets, sugary drinks and packaged juices for rare occasions.
- Strength training 2–3 times a week helps your body use insulin better.
