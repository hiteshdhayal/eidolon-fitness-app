- Take your thyroid tablet on an empty stomach, 30–60 minutes before breakfast.
- Keep calcium and iron tablets about 4 hours away from it.
- Have your tea or coffee after breakfast, not with the tablet.
- Your plan never changes your dose. Follow your doctor.
