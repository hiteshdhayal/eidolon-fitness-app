---
title: "High cholesterol, triglycerides or fatty liver"
appliesTo: ["condition:cholesterol", "condition:fattyLiver"]
priority: 35
reviewedBy: ""
---
- Limit fried food, vanaspati and other trans fats.
- Cut back on sugar and sweet drinks, and on alcohol.
- Eat more fibre: oats, dals, millets, vegetables and whole fruit.
