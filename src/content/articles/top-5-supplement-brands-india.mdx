Food comes first. For the plans I build, you do not need a supplement at all — dal, curd, paneer, chana and eggs do the work. This page is only for the moments when you are already eating well and want to check a brand properly.

I am still choosing the brands. Until I have checked each label and lab report myself, every card below is a placeholder. It is not a recommendation yet.

## What to check before you buy

- **Third-party testing.** Ask for a lab report you can read, not a badge on the front of the tub. A brand that hides it is telling you something.
- **The FSSAI licence number.** It should be printed on the label. No number, no buy.
- **A full ingredient list.** You should be able to see every ingredient and its dose.
- **No proprietary blends.** If the label groups ingredients under one name and hides the amounts, you cannot judge it.
- **Price per serving.** A big tub that costs more per serving is not a better deal.

## What this page is not

This is not medical advice, and none of these products treats or cures anything. A supplement cannot replace food, sleep or training. If a brand claims to fix a condition, close the page.
