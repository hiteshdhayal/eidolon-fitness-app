Your step count is the easiest activity number to track, and the one I use in the plan builder. You do not need a new device — your phone already counts.

## On an iPhone

Open the Health app, then Browse → Activity → Steps. Switch to the Week tab to see the daily bars and the average.

## On Android

Google Fit: open the app, tap the steps card, then the week view. Samsung Health: open Steps and choose the weekly tab. Both show a daily average.

## Use a weekly average

One busy day or one sick day tells you nothing. Take the last full week and use the average. If you are unsure, choose the band below your average rather than above it.

## What to do with it

Bring the average to the question "Your average daily steps" and pick the matching band. The plan adds about 2,000 steps and caps the target at 10,000, so the number you start with matters less than being honest about it.
