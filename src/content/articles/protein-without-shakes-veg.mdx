You can hit a sensible protein target on Indian vegetarian food. It takes a protein source at two or three meals, not one big portion at night.

## How much you need

Most adults do fine between 1.0 and 1.6 g of protein per kg of body weight. The plan builder sets your number from your goal, training and weight.

## Putting it together

- Breakfast: curd with poha, or besan chilla, or two eggs if you eat them.
- Lunch: dal or rajma with rice or roti, plus a katori of curd.
- Dinner: paneer, tofu or soya with a sabzi.
- Snack: roasted chana or sprouts.

## Do you need powder?

No. Powder is convenient when food is not enough or when you are travelling. It is not better than dal and curd.
