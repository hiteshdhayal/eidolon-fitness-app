PCOS is not about one forbidden food. It is about steady meals, enough protein and a slow, kind rate of fat loss.

- Eat at regular times. Long gaps and then a big meal make cravings worse.
- Build each meal around protein: dal, curd, paneer, chana, eggs or fish.
- Pick lower-GI grains more often: millets, whole wheat, oats, brown rice.
- Walk after meals when you can. Ten minutes helps more than it sounds.
- Do not cut calories hard. A slow deficit and strength training protect your muscle.
- Your doctor and a dietitian are still the main people for PCOS. Food supports the plan; it does not replace it.
