Supplements are a small part of this, and only after food. This reel explains how I check a brand before I would ever mention it.

- Third-party testing matters more than the front of the label. Look for a lab report you can actually read.
- The FSSAI licence number should be printed on the label. No number, no buy.
- A full ingredient list beats a "proprietary blend". You should know every dose.
- Price per serving is a better comparison than price per tub.
- Nothing here treats or cures a condition. If you take medicines, ask your doctor first.
