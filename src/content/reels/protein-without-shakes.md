Most people do not need a tub of powder. They need one more protein source at two meals a day. That is the whole idea of this reel.

- Dal, curd, paneer, chana and eggs cover most of it. A katori of cooked dal is about 7 g of protein; 100 g of paneer is about 18 g; two eggs are about 12 g.
- Spread it across the day. One big protein meal at night is harder to use than a little at breakfast, lunch and dinner.
- Breakfast is usually the weak spot. Add curd, eggs or besan chilla instead of just tea and toast.
- If you eat rice, pair it with dal or rajma rather than eating it alone.
- Powder is convenient, not magic. It helps when food is not enough, or when you are travelling.

The plan builder uses these foods to hit your protein target without supplements.
